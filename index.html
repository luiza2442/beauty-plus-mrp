Processing order: items are planned level by level (low-level coding): N3000 → N3300, N3700, N3800 → N3330, N3340, N3350, N3730, N3740, N3750 → N3353, N3357. A component is planned only after all its parents.

          
Gross requirements (GR): N3000 = customer demand. Component = Σ over parents of [parent planned order release in week t × quantity per × scrap factor], rounded up to the item's precision. Scrap factor = 1 + scrap% (or 1 ÷ (1 − scrap%) with the alternative rule).

          
Projected available (PA): PAt = PAt−1 + scheduled receiptst + planned order receiptst − GRt, starting from the initial inventory.

          
Net requirement (NR): NRt = max(0, GRt + safety stock − PAt−1 − scheduled receiptst).

          
Planned order receipt: lot-sizing rule applied to NRt (lot-for-lot, multiple of batch, or minimum batch).

          
Planned order release: the planned receipt offset by the lead time (released in week t − lead time). If that week is before week 1, the order is shown as LATE in the "Now" column: it must be released now and expedited.

          
Week 1 orders: all planned order releases in week 1 plus any late releases, split into purchased items (external suppliers) and manufactured items (in-house).

          
Assumptions: safety stock for finished goods is held by the distribution centers, so it is 0 by default; capacity is assumed sufficient; N3300 and N3700 lead time = 2 weeks (two 1-week operations in centers A and B); scheduled receipts entered for week 0 or earlier are treated as available now.
